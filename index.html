About Atena

Art Rooted in Emotion, Intuition & Transformation

Atena Salehi is a Toronto-based contemporary artist whose work explores identity, consciousness, transformation, and the inner human experience.

Her paintings are developed through an intuitive process influenced by meditation, Persian poetry, personal narrative, and the expressive power of colour and texture.

Raised in Isfahan and Tehran and trained at the University of Art and Architecture, Atena harmonizes timeless poetic reverence with bold contemporary spatial presence.

Artist Biography

Atena Salehi

Born in Isfahan and raised in Tehran, Atena Salehi grew up in an environment where creativity and education flourished. From early childhood, she was captivated by the vibrant colors of Persian vitrail and miniature artistry, and mesmerized by her father's free-style calligraphy.

Atena earned her formal education at the University of Art and Architecture in Tehran and taught painting at the Reza Abbasi Museum. Influenced by her aunt, a miniature artist and disciple of Master Farshchian, Atena's work weaves historical reverence with contemporary textural abstraction.

Now based in Toronto, Atena's paintings are spiritual explorations inspired by the poetry of Mawlana Mohammad Jalaluddin Balkhi (Rumi), meditation, and the human search for transcendence. Through Artestan Gallery, her works grace private collections and prime residential architectural spaces across Ontario.
